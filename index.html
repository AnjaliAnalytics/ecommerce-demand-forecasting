Business Context & Challenge

E-commerce retail operations suffer multi-million dollar losses caused by stockouts (lost revenue and degraded customer lifetime value) and overstocking (capital tied up in excess holding costs). Static reorder thresholds fail to adapt to demand volatility.

This project establishes a data-driven pipeline to forecast demand, quantify safety stock, automate dynamic reorder thresholds (ROP), and surface financial risk metrics across a multi-SKU retail portfolio.

Actionable Intelligence

Executive Findings & Action Plan

Core data insights mapped directly to operational procurement decisions.

FINDING #01
Out-of-Sample Forecast Stability
Problem Identified
High demand volatility caused complex models to overfit transient sales spikes.
Data Evidence
4-Week Moving Average achieved optimal out-of-sample WAPE of 50.34% and MAE of 325.50 units.
Business Action Plan
Adopt 4-week smoothed moving baseline for stable replenishment planning.
FINDING #02
Service Level Guarantee
Problem Identified
Arbitrary safety stock buffers led to frequent stockouts during lead-time spikes.
Data Evidence
Formulated statistical Z-score safety stock equation targeting a 95% Cycle Service Level.
Business Action Plan
Implement dynamic Safety Stock formulas based on weekly demand standard deviation.
FINDING #03
Dynamic Reorder Point (ROP) Triggers
Problem Identified
Fixed reorder points failed to account for supplier lead-time changes.
Data Evidence
Reorder Point equation mathematically tied ROP directly to Lead Time Demand + Safety Stock.
Business Action Plan
Automate purchase order triggers whenever Inventory Position drops below ROP.
FINDING #04
Stockout Exposure Prioritization
Problem Identified
Procurement teams lacked visibility into which SKUs required immediate capital allocation.
Data Evidence
Segmented product portfolio into 4 risk categories (`REORDER NOW`, `MONITOR`, `SUFFICIENT`, `OVERSTOCK`).
Business Action Plan
Direct 100% of critical procurement budgets immediately to `REORDER NOW` items.
FINDING #05
GenAI Executive Briefing Integration
Problem Identified
Manual compilation of C-suite inventory risk reports consumed significant analyst time.
Data Evidence
Integrated Gemini API (`gemini-3.6-flash`) with low temperature (0.2) grounding controls.
Business Action Plan
Automate factual executive summaries without risk of numerical hallucination.
FINDING #06
Streamlit Portfolio Deployment
Problem Identified
Stakeholders needed a lightweight, standalone UI to inspect SKU metrics on demand.
Data Evidence
Engineered fast interactive Streamlit app (`src/app.py`) reading precomputed Star Schema outputs.
Business Action Plan
Deploy Streamlit web UI for rapid cross-departmental inventory inspection.
